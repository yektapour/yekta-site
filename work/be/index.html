    About

    YEKTA

    YEKTA is the name Hossein Yektapour paints under — a digital painter who spent fifteen years as an art director, making images for others, before turning to his own.

    The work is conceptual and quiet: bodies that come apart, faces that turn into objects, and a red thread that keeps returning — the line that ties a thought to what it costs.

    overthink(paint)ing is how he names the process: thinking a feeling all the way through, until it has no choice but to become a picture.

    Alongside painting he makes interactive and generative work.
